### 📖 Lesson 27: Present Perfect Tense (El Pretérito Perfecto Compuesto)

**Subtitle**: Recent Actions, Life Experiences, and Auxiliary HABER  
**Professor's Note**: *"The Present Perfect bridges past actions with present relevance! Think of it as 'have done' in English."*

#### 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master conjugation of auxiliary verb *haber* (he, has, ha, hemos, han)
- Form regular past participles (-ar -> -ado, -er/-ir -> -ido)
- Memorize 10 key irregular past participles (*abierto, dicho, escrito, hecho, visto, vuelto, etc.*)

#### 📐 Grammar & Structural Rules

##### Auxiliary HABER & Past Participle Formation

The Present Perfect is formed by combining the present tense of **haber** with the past participle. Unlike English, **never** insert any words (pronouns or adverbs) between *haber* and the participle!

| Pronoun | Haber Conjugation | Regular -AR Participle | Regular -ER/-IR Participle | Full Sentence Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yo | **he** | hablado | comido | Yo he hablado con el profesor |
| Tú | **has** | trabajado | vivido | Tú has trabajado mucho |
| Él / Ella / Ud. | **ha** | viajado | aprendido | Ella ha aprendido español |
| Nosotros | **hemos** | cenado | asistido | Nosotros hemos cenado temprano |
| Ellos / Ellas / Uds. | **han** | ganado | salido | Ellos han salido a la calle |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Word Order with Pronouns**: Reflexive and object pronouns ALWAYS go before *haber*!  
> *Example*: **Me he levantado** a las siete. (NOT: *He me levantado*)

##### Irregular Past Participles Table

| Verb | Infinitive | Irregular Participle | Example Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Escribir | to write | **escrito** | Hemos escrito un informe. |
| Hacer | to do/make | **hecho** | ¿Qué has hecho hoy? |
| Morir | to die | **muerto** | El gato ha muerto. |

### 📖 Lesson 28: Simple Future Tense (El Futuro Simple)

**Subtitle**: Intentions, Predictions, and Irregular Stems  
**Professor's Note**: *"Unlike present tense where endings vary by verb family, the simple future attaches endings directly to the full infinitive for -ar, -er, AND -ir verbs!"*

#### 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master future endings (-é, -ás, -á, -emos, -éis, -án) on full infinitives
- Memorize the 12 irregular future stems (*tendr-, pondr-, saldr-, vendr-, podr-, sabr-, habr-, har-, dir-*)
- Express probability in the present (*¿Qué hora será? - Serán las tres*)

#### 📐 Grammar & Structural Rules

##### Regular Future Tense Endings

Attach these endings directly to the full infinitive of **-ar, -er, and -ir** verbs:

| Pronoun | Ending | Hablar (to speak) | Comer (to eat) | Vivir (to live) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yo | **-é** | hablaré | comeré | viviré |
| Tú | **-ás** | hablarás | comerás | vivirás |
| Él / Ella / Ud. | **-á** | hablará | comerá | vivirá |
| Nosotros | **-emos** | hablaremos | comeremos | viviremos |
| Ellos / Ellas / Uds. | **-án** | hablarán | comerán | vivirán |

> [!TIP]
> Notice that all endings except *nosotros (-emos)* carry a written accent mark!

##### Irregular Stems Grouping

Irregular verbs keep the standard endings, but alter their root stem:

1. **Drop Infinitive Vowel**: Caber -> *cabr-*, Haber -> *habr-*, Poder -> *podr-*, Saber -> *sabr-*, Querer -> *querr-*
2. **Drop Vowel & Add 'D'**: Poner -> *pondr-*, Salir -> *saldr-*, Tener -> *tendr-*, Venir -> *vendr-*, Valer -> *valdr-*
3. **Shortened Stems**: Hacer -> *har-*, Decir -> *dir-*

| Infinitive | Irregular Stem | Yo Conjugation | Nosotros Conjugation | Example Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tener | **tendr-** | yo tendré | nosotros tendremos | Tendré tiempo mañana. |
| Poner | **pondr-** | yo pondré | nosotros pondremos | Pondré la mesa. |
| Salir | **saldr-** | yo saldré | nosotros saldremos | Saldremos a las seis. |
| Hacer | **har-** | yo haré | nosotros haremos | Haré la cena hoy. |
| Decir | **dir-** | yo diré | nosotros diremos | Diré la verdad. |

### 📖 Lesson 29: Conditional Tense (El Condicional Simple)

**Subtitle**: Hypothetical Scenarios, Polite Requests, and Future-in-the-Past  
**Professor's Note**: *"The conditional is your go-to tense for polite requests ('Me gustaría...') and hypothetical situations ('Viajaría si tuviera dinero')."*

#### 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Apply conditional endings (-ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían) to infinitives
- Use identical irregular stems as future tense (*tendría, pondría, habría, haría, diría*)
- Soften requests and express probability in the past (*Serían las cinco cuando llegó*)

#### 📐 Grammar & Structural Rules

##### Regular Conditional Tense Endings

Attach these endings directly to the full infinitive of **-ar, -er, AND -ir** verbs:

| Pronoun | Ending | Viajar (to travel) | Comer (to eat) | Escribir (to write) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yo | **-ía** | viajaría | comería | escribiría |
| Tú | **-ías** | viajarías | comerías | escribirías |
| Él / Ella / Ud. | **-ía** | viajaría | comería | escribiría |
| Nosotros | **-íamos** | viajaríamos | comeríamos | escribiríamos |
| Ellos / Ellas / Uds. | **-ían** | viajarían | comerían | escribirían |

> [!NOTE]
> Every single form in the conditional tense carries a written accent mark over the **í**!

##### Irregular Conditional Stems (Same as Future Tense)

| Infinitive | Irregular Stem | Yo Conditional | Nosotros Conditional | Example Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tener | **tendr-** | yo tendría | nosotros tendríamos | Yo tendría más paciencia. |
| Poner | **pondr-** | yo pondría | nosotros pondríamos | Pondríamos la mesa aquí. |
| Salir | **saldr-** | yo saldría | nosotros saldríamos | Saldríamos de paseo hoy. |
| Hacer | **har-** | yo haría | nosotros haríamos | ¿Qué harías tú en mi lugar? |
| Decir | **dir-** | yo diría | nosotros diríamos | Yo diría que sí. |
| Poder | **podr-** | yo podría | nosotros podríais | Podríamos ir al cine. |

### 📖 Lesson 30: Intro to Present Subjunctive (El Presente de Subjuntivo)

**Subtitle**: WEIRDOS Triggers, Yo-Form Roots, and Opposite Vowel Endings  
**Professor's Note**: *"The subjunctive mood expresses subjectivity, doubt, wishes, and recommendations rather than objective facts!"*

#### 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Understand WEIRDOS triggers (Wishes, Emotions, Impersonal expressions, Requests, Doubt, Ojalá, Speculation)
- Derive present subjunctive from 1st person singular present indicative (*yo*) root
- Apply opposite vowel endings (-ar verbs take -e endings; -er/-ir verbs take -a endings)

#### 📐 Grammar & Structural Rules

##### The 3-Step Formula for Present Subjunctive

To form the present subjunctive for regular and stem-changing verbs:

1. Start with the **yo** form of the present indicative: *hablo, como, escribo, tengo, hago*
2. Drop the **-o** ending: *habl-, com-, escrib-, teng-, hag-*
3. Add the **OPPOSITE** vowel endings!

##### Opposite Vowel Endings Table

| Pronoun | -AR Verbs (take -E endings) | -ER & -IR Verbs (take -A endings) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yo | **-e** (hable) | **-a** (coma / viva) |
| Tú | **-es** (hables) | **-as** (comas / vivas) |
| Él / Ella / Ud. | **-e** (hable) | **-a** (coma / viva) |
| Nosotros | **-emos** (hablemos) | **-amos** (comamos / vivamos) |
| Ellos / Ellas / Uds. | **-en** (hablen) | **-an** (coman / vivan) |

##### The WEIRDOS Subjunctive Triggers

The subjunctive is required in dependent clauses after **que** when the main clause expresses:

- **W**ishes / Will: *Quiero que, Deseo que, Necesito que*
- **E**motions: *Me alegra que, Siento que, Me gusta que*
- **I**mpersonal Expressions: *Es importante que, Es necesario que, Es bueno que*
- **R**equests / Recommendations: *Recomiendo que, Pido que, Sugiero que*
- **D**oubt / Denial: *Dudo que, No creo que, No pienso que*
- **O**jalá: *¡Ojalá que...!* (God willing / I hope that)
- **S**peculation / Future Conjunctions: *Para que, Antes de que, En cuanto*

#### 🔤 Vocabulary Table

| Spanish Term | Phonetic Pronunciation | English Meaning | Usage Context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **quiera** | `KYEH-rah` | s/he wants (subjunctive) | Subjunctive of *querer* |
| **hable** | `AH-bleh` | s/he speaks (subjunctive) | Subjunctive of *hablar* |
| **coma** | `KOH-mah` | s/he eats (subjunctive) | Subjunctive of *comer* |
| **viva** | `BEE-bah` | s/he lives (subjunctive) | Subjunctive of *vivir* |
| **tenga** | `TEHN-gah` | s/he has (subjunctive) | Subjunctive of *tener* |
| **haga** | `AH-gah` | s/he does/makes (subjunctive) | Subjunctive of *hacer* |
| **dudo que** | `DOO-doh keh` | I doubt that | Doubt trigger |
| **ojalá** | `oh-hah-LAH` | hopefully / I wish | Universal hope trigger |

#### 💬 Core Example Sentences

1. **Spanish**: `Quiero que tú estudies español en la escuela cada día.`  
   **English**: I want you to study Spanish at school every day.  
   *Breakdown*: Wish trigger *Quiero que* + subjunctive *estudies*.

2. **Spanish**: `Es importante que nosotros comamos fruta en la cocina todas las mañanas.`  
   **English**: It is important that we eat fruit in the kitchen every morning.  
   *Breakdown*: Impersonal trigger *Es importante que* + subjunctive *comamos*.

3. **Spanish**: `Dudo que él tenga tiempo en la oficina hoy.`  
   **English**: I doubt that he has time at the office today.  
   *Breakdown*: Doubt trigger *Dudo que* + subjunctive *tenga* (from *tengo*).

### 📖 Lesson 31: DISCO Subjunctive Irregulars & Stem Changers

**Subtitle**: DISCO Verbs, Orthographic Shifts, and Advanced Subjunctive Mechanics  
**Professor's Note**: *"Verbs that lack an -o in the 'yo' form of present indicative cannot use the 3-step rule! Remember DISCO: Dar, Ir, Ser, Caber/Conocer, Oír/Saber!"*

#### 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master DISCO irregulars (*dar -> dé, ir -> vaya, ser -> sea, haber -> haya, estar -> esté, saber -> sepa*)
- Master spelling changes preserving sound (*-car -> -que, -gar -> -gue, -zar -> -ce*)
- Apply stem-changing rules in subjunctive for -ar/-er vs -ir verbs (*nosotros durmamos, sintamos*)

#### 📐 Grammar & Structural Rules

##### The DISCO Irregular Verbs Table

Verbs that are completely irregular in present indicative *yo* form take unique subjunctive roots:

| Verb | Yo Indicative | Subjunctive Root / Forms | Full Subjunctive Conjugation (yo, tú, él, nosotros, ellos) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dar** (to give) | doy | d- | dé, des, dé, demos, den |
| **Ir** (to go) | voy | vay- | vaya, vayas, vaya, vayamos, vayan |
| **Ser** (to be) | soy | se- | sea, seas, sea, seamos, sean |
| **Estar** (to be) | estoy | est- | esté, estés, esté, estemos, estén |
| **Saber** (to know) | sé | sep- | sepa, sepas, sepa, sepamos, sepan |
| **Haber** (there to be) | he | hay- | haya, hayas, haya, hayamos, hayan |

##### Orthographic Spelling Changes in Subjunctive

To preserve original consonant sounds before subjunctive **-e** or **-a** endings:

- **-CAR -> -QUE**: *Buscar -> busque, busques, busque, busquemos, busquen*
- **-GAR -> -GUE**: *Llegar -> llegue, llegues, llegue, lleguemos, lleguen*
- **-ZAR -> -CE**: *Empezar -> empiece, empieces, empiece, empecemos, empiecen*

##### Stem Changes in Subjunctive (-AR/-ER vs -IR)

- **-AR / -ER Verbs**: Stem changes occur in all forms EXCEPT *nosotros*: *pensar -> piense, pienses, piense, **pensemos**, piensen*.
- **-IR Verbs**: Stem changes occur in ALL forms, including *nosotros* (e->i, o->u): *dormir -> duerma, duermas, duerma, **durmamos**, duerman*; *sentir -> sienta, sientas, sienta, **sintamos**, sientan*.
